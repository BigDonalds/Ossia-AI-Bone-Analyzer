# Ossia privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy covers the Ossia app for Android and iOS. The same text is in the
app, under Settings → Privacy policy.

## In short

Ossia collects nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads, no usage
statistics and no tracking, and nothing is shared with anyone.

## Photos and reports

Photos you take or pick are analyzed on your phone. Reports you save, with
their photos and any names or notes you add, stay inside Ossia on your phone.
Deleting a report, or the app, deletes them. A report only goes to your
clipboard when you choose to copy it.

## Online analysis

Online analysis is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the
photos you analyze, and a length if you typed one, are sent to the address you
entered: a computer you or your group run (the server in `training/` of this
repository). The Ossia developer doesn't run that computer and never receives
your photos.

## What Ossia asks for

- **Camera**: to photograph a bone.
- **Photos**: Ossia only sees the photos you pick.
- **Local network**: to reach your computer for Online analysis, if your phone
  asks.

Ossia never records sound and never uses your location.

## Children

Ossia is not aimed at children, and it collects no personal information from
anyone, children included.
